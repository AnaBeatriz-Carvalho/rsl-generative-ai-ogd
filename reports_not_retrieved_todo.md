# Reports Not Retrieved — status after the additional retrieval round (10 studies)

During the iSys major revision, an **additional full-text retrieval round** was conducted for all **19** reports
originally classified as *reports not retrieved* (PRISMA 2020). Using, as applicable, publisher/DOI pages,
institutional access, alternative versions indexed by scholarly search engines, ResearchGate/repositories, and
direct author requests, **9 full texts were recovered**. The original eligibility criteria (CI1–CI5, CE1–CE5) were
re-applied without modification: **7 were included** and **2 were excluded after full-text assessment**. **10 remain
not retrieved** and stay recorded as *reports not retrieved*.

> **Integrity note (updated).** The corpus moved from **29 to 36** studies **only** because of this real,
> documented recovery round. The 7 newly included studies were extracted, quality-assessed and classified with the
> **same** instrument used for the original corpus, from their full texts. The 10 below were **not** recovered and
> remain a source of *availability bias* (Threats to Validity): their later inclusion could still shift model,
> venue, or geographic distributions.

Recovery routes attempted per item: (1) publisher page via the DOI; (2) institutional / CAPES access;
(3) author self-archived / preprint / ResearchGate / institutional repository; (4) direct e-mail to the
corresponding author; (5) Google Scholar "All versions".
